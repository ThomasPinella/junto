# 11. Optional meeting videos

Meeting recordings can initially be handled through unlisted YouTube videos.

An administrator:

1. uploads a recording manually to YouTube as unlisted;
2. pastes the YouTube URL into the meeting editor;
3. the application validates the URL;
4. the meeting page displays the embedded player.

The application stores:

- meeting ID;
- Junto ID;
- YouTube video ID;
- title;
- optional description;
- added-by user;
- creation timestamp.

Important limitation:

Unlisted YouTube videos are not truly access-controlled. Anyone with the URL can view or reshare them.

The embedded recording should therefore be presented only within the authenticated member portal, but members must understand that the underlying link can still be copied.

No direct video upload or video transcoding is required in the MVP.
