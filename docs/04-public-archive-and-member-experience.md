# Public Archive and Member Experience

This document covers the editorial public experience, the focused private portal, and optional meeting recordings.

## 7. Public archive

The public archive should be attractive enough that authors feel proud sharing it.

### Homepage

The public homepage can show:

- Junto name and description;
- recent public essays;
- featured essays;
- recent meetings;
- participating authors;
- brief explanation of the Junto practice.

### Essay archive

Visitors can browse public essays by:

- most recent;
- author;
- meeting;
- potentially topic or tag later.

### Essay reading page

A public essay page should display:

Essay title
Optional subtitle
Author name
Meeting date and meeting title
Estimated reading time
Essay body
Discussion
About the author
More from this author
Other essays from this meeting

The reading experience should avoid unnecessary sidebars, dashboards, and application chrome.

### Author page

A public author page includes:

- display name;
- optional profile photo;
- optional short biography;
- public essays;
- meetings in which the author published public essays.

A member’s private essays must not affect public counts or previews.

### Meeting page

A public meeting page includes:

- meeting date;
- title or theme;
- public description;
- public essays from the meeting;
- authors who published publicly.

Members-only essays must not be revealed or counted publicly.

## 10. Member portal

The member portal should remain simple and focused.

### Portal navigation

Home
Meetings
Essays
Chat
Members
Profile

Admins additionally see:

Admin

### Portal home

The home view can show:

- next meeting;
- essay deadline;
- essays submitted for the next meeting;
- recent comments on the member’s essays;
- recent chat activity;
- button to write an essay.

### Essay dashboard

Members can see:

- drafts;
- published essays;
- meeting association;
- visibility;
- last edited time;
- public URL where applicable.

Useful actions:

Write essay
Edit
Preview
Publish
Change visibility
View discussion

### Meeting view

The authenticated meeting view includes:

- all public and members-only published essays for that meeting;
- submission status by member, if desired;
- meeting details;
- optional recording;
- links into essay discussions.

### Members page

The members page can show:

- active members;
- display names;
- profile photos;
- short bios;
- role within the Junto.

Email addresses should not be displayed to other members by default.

## 11. Optional meeting videos

Meeting recordings can initially be handled through unlisted YouTube videos.

An administrator:

1. uploads a recording manually to YouTube as unlisted;
2. pastes the YouTube URL into the meeting editor;
3. the application validates the URL;
4. the meeting page displays the embedded player.

The application stores:

- meeting ID;
- Junto ID;
- YouTube video ID;
- title;
- optional description;
- added-by user;
- creation timestamp.

Important limitation:

Unlisted YouTube videos are not truly access-controlled. Anyone with the URL can view or reshare them.

The embedded recording should therefore be presented only within the authenticated member portal, but members must understand that the underlying link can still be copied.

No direct video upload or video transcoding is required in the MVP.
