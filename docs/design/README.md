# Design

Junto’s design direction is a **contemporary civic journal**: a living publication made around a table, with the permanence of an archive and the intimacy of an in-person gathering.

The design should be literary without becoming precious, warm without becoming lifestyle-oriented, and historically conscious without using faux-antique imagery. Essays remain the central object. Meetings give the archive its rhythm and context.

## Documents

- [Visual system and tokens](../../DESIGN.md) — normative colors, typography, spacing, component posture, rationale, and design constraints. This is the primary source of truth for implementation.
- [Surface design guidelines](./surface-guidelines.md) — practical guidance for the homepage, archive, essay pages, meetings, authors, discussion, member portal, editor, chat, imagery, motion, and responsive behavior.

## Primary approved direction

The primary visual reference is the first generated homepage concept:

- all-caps **JUNTO** masthead;
- warm paper canvas;
- ink-led typography;
- restrained oxblood accent;
- meeting-led editorial composition;
- essay listings built with type, whitespace, alignment, and fine rules rather than SaaS cards;
- sparse, authentic documentary imagery from the practice.

The essay-page concept is a secondary reference for long-form reading: narrow measure, generous margins, minimal chrome, visible meeting context, and discussion treated as thoughtful marginalia rather than social media.

## Creative brief

> Design Junto as a living civic journal made around a table: intellectually serious, materially warm, quietly contemporary, and centered on authored thought rather than online activity.
